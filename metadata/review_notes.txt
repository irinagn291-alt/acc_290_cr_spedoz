The app requests no permissions. No permission prompt appears, and there is no usage-description string.

There is no login, no in-app purchase, no user-generated content, no account deletion flow, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt. “Erase every mark and the limit” only clears the local chart; there is no account to delete.

Runout is a personal daily counter. You file one unit at a time so today's measure stays inside a Daily limit you choose, and you watch the needle on the cluster. It is for anyone who overruns a soft daily cap and wants the bracing band to change the next action — hold Seat, then Tick — not only to change a colour.

No credentials or demo account are needed. A cold launch on a device that has never finished onboarding shows three pages with no tab bar. Page 1 headline “Seat the bezel, then tick the day”, line “Runout keeps today's measure inside a limit you choose.”, buttons “Next” and “Skip”. Page 2 headline “Hold Seat, then Tick”, line “From the bracing band, a two second hold files a seat and opens one Tick.”, buttons “Next” and “Skip”. Page 3 headline “The cluster stays on this device”, line “Calendar and charts read the same marks. Nothing leaves the phone.”, buttons “Continue” and “Skip”. “Next” advances one page. “Continue” or “Skip” ends onboarding with Daily limit 10 and Unit label “units”, and opens the cluster. Home is only “Nothing logged today”, “Tick once to start today's measure inside the limit.”, and “Tick” until the first Tick; Calendar, Charts, History, Settings, and “How bracing works” cannot be opened yet. Tap “Tick” to reveal the cluster (no title, no tab bar): “Keep today's measure inside the limit”, “1 of 10 units”, needle “Bracing”, next-tap line, “Tick”, “Seat”, “How bracing works”, “Calendar”, “Charts”, “History”, and “Settings”. Hold “Seat” for two seconds (spoken “Seat. Hold for two seconds.”); a short tap does nothing, and “Seat” is faded until the bracing band (85 percent of Daily limit; 8 when the limit is 10). In the bracing band, “Tick” without a Seat files a Wobble (no unit added, wobble count appears, cluster dims once). Hold “Seat”, then “Tick”, to add a unit. After a Seat: “Seat is filed. Next tap: Tick, one unit.” Only two seats are allowed in one day; later Ticks only file wobbles. At the Daily limit: “Today is at the limit. Tick stays closed until tomorrow.” and “Tick” is faded. Sheets have a grabber and X “Close”; swipe down also dismisses. Use “Calendar” and “Charts” (“Back to Tick”), “History”, “How bracing works”, and “Settings” (“Limit”, “Care”, “Contact”). “Daily limit” and “Unit label” save on leaving the field; “Done” dismisses the keyboard. “Show onboarding again” shows the three pages without wiping marks. “Erase every mark and the limit” shows “Erase the chart?” with “Keep marks” and “Erase chart”. “Contact Runout” opens the support page. On a physical iPhone or iPad the chart is empty after onboarding. On the iOS Simulator a first launch can skip onboarding and show “84 of 100 units” and “Next tap: Tick. That unit enters the bracing band and asks for Seat.” Portrait only on iPhone and iPad.

Onboarding states “The cluster stays on this device” and “Nothing leaves the phone.” Marks, the Daily limit, and the Unit label stay on this device. The on-screen control is “Contact Runout”; it opens the support page.

Copy does not change by region. Weekday initials, medium dates, short times, grouping separators, and percents follow the device calendar and locale.
