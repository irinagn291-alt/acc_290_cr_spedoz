Thank you for reviewing Runout. The build under review was retested on a clean device. Nothing we tried reproduced a crash, dead end, empty screen, or non-functioning feature.

Three behaviours are deliberate.

1. “Calendar” is this month only. There is no control to move to another month. A day that only has ordinary Ticks looks the same as an empty day until you tap it. “Back to Tick” closes the sheet.

2. Lowering “Daily limit” below today's already-filed total immediately shows “Today is at the limit. Tick stays closed until tomorrow.” and fades “Tick”. That is the cap applying at once.

3. A blank, zero, or non-digit “Daily limit”, or a blank “Unit label”, snaps back to the previous value when you leave the field. The value does not change.
